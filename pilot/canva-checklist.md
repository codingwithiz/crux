# Carousel Checklist (Ritual Step 6 — Express)

The carousel expresses a thesis you **already committed** in the ledger. It is the exhaust, not the thinking. **Don't open Canva until you have a defended opinion.**

## One-time Canva setup (~10 min)
1. Create a free Canva account; start a **Brand Kit** — 2 fonts, 3 colors, your handle/logo. Consistency = recognizability.
2. Build **one** reusable carousel template at **1080×1350** (portrait works for both IG and LinkedIn). Duplicate it each time.
3. Keep it boring and legible: big type, one idea per slide, high contrast. **Skip generic AI-art backgrounds** — they *reduce* reach in 2026.

## POV-carousel structure (5–7 slides)
1. **Hook** — your sharpest, most specific claim (the *variant view*). Not "X was released" but "Why X means Y." Make a scroll-stopping promise.
2. **Context** — what happened, in one line, for people who missed it.
3. **The conventional take** — what most people are saying (the consensus you're departing from).
4. **Your argument** — your evidence and reasoning (1–2 slides). Show the receipts.
5. **Steelman + rebuttal** — name the strongest counter, then answer it. This is what makes you look credible, not just loud.
6. **So what** — the implication / what to watch / what you'd do about it.
7. **CTA** — one line + your handle (e.g., "Follow for AI takes that survive scrutiny").

## Pre-post gut check
- Would I defend **slide 1** in a reply-guy fight? If not, sharpen or soften it.
- Is there a **real opinion** here, or did I just summarize? If it reads like TLDR, it's slop — go back to the ledger.
- Did I **credit/link** the source?
